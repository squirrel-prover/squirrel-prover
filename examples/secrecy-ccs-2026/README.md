This repository contains the case studies for the paper
"Secrecy in Squirrel and the Post-Compromise Security of a Ratchet",
Clément Hérouard, Charlie Jacomme, Adrien Koutsos, Joseph Lallemand, 
CCS 2026.

The case studies include:
- `asym-ratchet/`: the asymmetric ratchet of Section 5 (c.f.
  corresponding README);
- `motivating-example.sp`: the model and proof
   for the motivating example of Section 2;
-  `weak_secrecy_tuto.sp`: a small introductory tutorial teaching
   basic manipulations of the secrecy predicate.
